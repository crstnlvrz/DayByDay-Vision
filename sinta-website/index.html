Our Transformation

From the legacy of The Observatory to the birth of Sintá, we continue to blend art, tranquility, and fine cuisine in an unforgettable dining experience.

Nestled in the scenic Mystic Hills of Ligaya Drive, Talisay, Batangas, Sintá offers a secluded sanctuary where every dish tells a story and every moment becomes a memory.
